Wokku Cloud is in **public beta** and isn't meant for production workloads yet.

## What beta means

- The platform works for real projects, but bugs and short outages can happen while we ship updates.
- There's no uptime SLA yet.
- The API is `v1`. We may add fields, but we try not to break existing ones.

## Good fits right now

- Side projects, demos, and portfolios
- Internal tools
- Staging and preview environments
- Trying Wokku out before you move bigger workloads

Hold off on revenue-critical apps, apps with strict uptime needs, and compliance-sensitive data.

## What you can count on

- **Your data is yours.** You can [download database backups](/docs/databases/backups) and leave at any time. Your code stays in your own git repo.
- **Clear pricing.** You pay only for the resources you run. Up to 3 sleeping apps are free. See [Pricing](/docs/billing/pricing).
- **Secure basics.** Traffic is served over HTTPS, and database backups are encrypted.

Keep your own copy of anything you can't afford to lose.

## Report a problem

Email [support@wokku.cloud](mailto:support@wokku.cloud). Include your app name and what you saw. The **Support** link at the bottom of the dashboard sidebar opens the same address.
